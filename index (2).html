Good support values depend on your data: small datasets can use 5% or more, large stores often use 0.5–2%. If you get no rules, lower the support first.

#If customer buysThen may also buy Support ⇅Confidence ⇅Lift ⇅

Support = share of all transactions that contain both sides. Confidence = of the transactions with the "if" items, the share that also have the "then" items. Lift = confidence ÷ how common the "then" items are overall: above 1 means a real positive link, about 1 means no link, below 1 means a negative link.
